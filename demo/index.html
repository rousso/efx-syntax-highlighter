EFX editor

The editor holds one kind of EFX text, in the EFX colours. While you type, it offers the words the grammar takes at the cursor, the functions as calls; Ctrl+Space asks for them anywhere, template text too. The field and node ids it offers are those the text uses. It is a plain CodeMirror editor, with no undo history and no key bindings beyond the completion's: the package depends on no more of CodeMirror.
